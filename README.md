## About

I am a data scientist and sports analytics practitioner with a background in finance and an M.S. in Analytics from Georgia Tech. My work focuses on transforming complex data into actionable insights through machine learning, statistical modeling, optimization, and visualization.

I have developed analytics projects for organizations including DC United, the United States Olympic & Paralympic Committee (USOPC), and Houston Dynamo FC, with experience spanning player valuation, recruitment analysis, performance forecasting, and decision support systems.

### [Bayesian Possession Value Model](https://github.com/kshah-340/bayesian-possession-value)
A Bayesian framework for estimating how individual actions contribute to possession success in soccer. Built using StatsBomb event data and hierarchical Bayesian modeling techniques.

**Skills**: Bayesian Statistics, PyMC, Soccer Analytics, Feature Engineering, Data Visualization
